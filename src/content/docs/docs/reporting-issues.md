---
title: "Reporting Issues"
description: "How to report a bug."
---

Post bugs in **#bug-reports** on the [Discord](https://hippos-linux.org/discord). One problem per post.

## Include

- **HippOS version**: **Main Menu** → **System Settings** → **Information**
- **Device**: PC model or CPU and GPU, handheld model, or Pi model
- **What you did, what you expected, and what happened**
- **Whether it happens every time**
- For a game: the system, the game, and the emulator (hold **A** on the game → **Advanced Game Options** → **Emulator**)
- Photos or screenshots if they help (**Hotkey** + **L1** takes a screenshot)

## Logs

If staff need logs to track a problem down, they'll tell you how to get them.

## Not sure it's a bug?

Ask in the **Support** forum for your device first.
