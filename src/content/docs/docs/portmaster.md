---
title: "PortMaster"
description: "Install and play native ports of PC games with PortMaster."
---

[PortMaster](https://portmaster.games/) installs ready-made ports of PC games, many of them free and the rest using your own copy of the game's data files. HippOS comes with PortMaster installed.

## Installing ports

1. Open **Ports** in EmulationStation and start **PortMaster**.
2. Pick a port and install it.
3. Close PortMaster. The new port appears in **Ports**.

Ports that need the original game's files say so in PortMaster, along with where to put them (inside the port's folder in `/userdata/roms/ports/`).

## Playing

Ports start like any other game. **Hotkey** + **Start** quits a port. Ports and their files belong to your user, so nothing needs special permissions.

## Updates

PortMaster updates itself and your installed ports from its own menu.
