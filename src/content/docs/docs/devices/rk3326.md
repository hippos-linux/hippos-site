:::caution
RK3326 support is experimental. It's tested on the original R36S. If your screen stays black after the steps below, tell us in **#rk3326** on the [Discord](https://hippos-linux.org/discord) which one you have.
:::

## Installing

Write the **RK3326** image to a microSD card of 16 GB or more, set up the screen below, then put the card in the **first** card slot and switch on.

## Set up the screen

After writing, the card shows up on your computer as a small drive.

- **Odroid Go 2/3/Super, Anbernic RG351M/RG351V, Powkiddy RGB10, GameForce Chi, MagicX XU10**: nothing to do.
- **R36S and its clones**: screens differ from unit to unit. Open the **BOOT** partition of the handheld's original card and copy its `.dtb` file (for example `rk3326-r36s-linux.dtb`) into the `stock` folder on the HippOS card. Copy only one file, and leave out files ending in `-uboot.dtb`. On the first boot the screen stays blank while HippOS sets it up, then it restarts by itself.
- **R33S, EE clone, MagicX XU-Mini M, BatleXP G350 and XiFan models**: copy your model's file from the `devices` folder to the top of the card and rename the copy to `fdtfile.txt`. `devices/README.txt` lists which file is which. If an EE clone's screen stays blank, also copy the `.dtb` as for the R36S.

## Controls

- **FN** is the **Hotkey** button: **FN** + **Start** exits a game, **FN** + **A** opens the quick menu. See [Controllers](/docs/controllers/#hotkeys).
- The volume buttons on the side change the volume.
- The power button switches the handheld off.

## Games on the second card

Leave your games on a card in the **second** slot. HippOS merges its `roms`, `bios` and `saves` folders into your library, including cards set up by other handheld systems: folder names such as `genesis` or `sfc` are understood. See [Storage](/docs/storage/).

## Wi-Fi and SSH

The R36S has no Wi-Fi of its own; use a USB Wi-Fi adapter on the OTG port. SSH is off by default on this image to save power: **Main Menu** → **System Settings** → **Services**.

## Battery and heat

The battery level shows in EmulationStation. Games run at full speed (up to 1.3 GHz), and HippOS keeps the chip within a power budget, easing its speed off as it nears 85 °C. Long sessions of demanding systems may slow down a little as it warms up, and it gets warm to the touch; that's normal.

## What runs

Systems up to PlayStation and most handheld systems run; check each system's page under [Supported Systems](/docs/systems/) for **Raspberry Pi and RK3326 handhelds**.
