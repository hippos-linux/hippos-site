---
title: "Raspberry Pi"
description: "HippOS on the Raspberry Pi 4 and 5."
---

The **Raspberry Pi** image runs on the Raspberry Pi 4 and 5. Write it to a microSD card or USB drive of 32 GB or more and switch the Pi on; it boots from the card or drive by itself.

## What to expect

- Classic consoles, handhelds and PlayStation run well, especially on a Pi 5.
- Lighter PlayStation 2 and Wii U games run on a Pi 5; newer systems are hit and miss.
- Xbox, Xbox 360 and PlayStation 3 aren't available: the Pi can't run them.
- Each system's page says whether it runs on the Pi (**Runs on**), and which emulator is the default there.

## Storage

A fast card (A2 rated) or a USB SSD makes loading noticeably quicker. Games can also stay on a USB drive or network share, see [Storage](/docs/storage/).

## Cooling

A Pi 5 slows itself down when it gets hot. Use a case with a fan or the active cooler for demanding systems.
