## About

The SC-3000 is a computer by Sega. On HippOS it runs on PC, Raspberry Pi and RK3326 handhelds, with ares as the default emulator.

## Getting ROMs

Put your files in `/userdata/roms/sc3000/`. See [Adding ROMs](/docs/adding-roms/) for ways to copy them to HippOS.

After adding games: **Main Menu** → **Game Settings** → **Update Gamelists**.

## Emulators

| Emulator (★ default) | About | PC | Pi / RK3326 |
|---|---|:-:|:-:|
| **[ares](https://ares-emu.net/)** | Multi-system emulator focused on accuracy, from the authors of higan.<br><small>**Formats:** all · **BIOS:** none</small> | ★ | ★ |
| **[MAME](https://www.mamedev.org/)** | Multi-system arcade and computer emulator (GroovyMAME build, with CRT support).<br><small>**Formats:** all · **BIOS:** none</small> | ✔ | ✔ |

## Game Formats Explained

- **.7z**: 7-Zip archive; works like .zip but is slower to open.
- **.wav**: Audio recording of a tape.
- **.zip**: Zip archive. Most cartridge systems and arcade sets can be played zipped.
