---
title: "RetroAchievements"
description: "Earn achievements in classic games."
---

[RetroAchievements](https://retroachievements.org/) adds achievements, leaderboards and rich presence to classic games.

**Main Menu** → **System Settings** → **RetroAchievement Settings**

1. Turn RetroAchievements on and enter your **Username** and **Password**.
2. Optional: **Hardcore mode** (no save states, rewind or slow motion, for ranked play), **Leaderboards**, **Rich presence**, the unlock sound and an automatic screenshot when you earn one.

Achievements work in RetroArch and in standalone emulators with their own support. Your progress shows in **RetroAchievements** in the main menu.
