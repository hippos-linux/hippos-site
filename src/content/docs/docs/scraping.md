**Main Menu** → **Scraper**

The scraper downloads box art, screenshots, videos, logos and descriptions for your games.

1. Under **Included scrapers**, pick the sources to use.
2. Under **Filters**, choose which games to scrape: for example only **Games missing any media**, and skip recently scraped games.
3. Choose the systems and start.

## Accounts

Some sources have limits for anonymous use. Add your own account under **Accounts** (ScreenScraper, IGDB) for faster scraping.

## Media

Choose which images to use (box front, 3D box, screenshot and so on) in the scraper's media settings. Your theme decides where each one is shown.
