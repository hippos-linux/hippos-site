title: "Updates"
description: "System and emulator updates, and rolling back."
---

HippOS updates over the internet. Updates replace the system only: your games, saves and settings in `/userdata` are kept.

## Updating

**Main Menu** → **Updates & Downloads** → **Start Update**

The list shows what's available:

- **HippOS**: a new system version. It's applied on the next reboot.
- **EmulationStation**
- **Emulators**: emulator updates come out between system releases. Update them one by one or all at once.

**Check for Updates** in the same menu turns the automatic check on or off.

## Something broke?

**Main Menu** → **Updates & Downloads** → **Rollback to previous version** goes back to the version you had before.

## What changed

Each release's changes are on the [changelog](https://hippos-linux.org/changelog) and in **#update-log** on the [Discord](https://hippos-linux.org/discord).
