### Updates

- Updates now refuse to start if they won't fit on the disk, and old deployments are pruned before a new one downloads.
- Fixed rollback and kernel-snapshot handling in the updater.

### Display & session

- The 1080p60 safe default resolution now actually applies.
- Desktop display layouts are kept across monitor hotplug.
- Logging out of desktop mode returns to EmulationStation, and EmulationStation hotkeys no longer fire inside the desktop.

### Diagnostics

- Logs can be uploaded straight from EmulationStation, with a token entry field in the menu.

### Emulators

- Rebuilt PCSX2, RPCS3 and Ymir.
- DOSBox Staging is now built with CMake and vcpkg.
